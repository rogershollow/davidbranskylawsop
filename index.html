Purpose

This SOP standardizes how the paralegal drafts, organizes, and files the core documents in a Bransky Law family law matter. Sections 2–3 apply to every task; Sections 4–11 are the individual workflows; Section 12 lists firm-wide standards; Section 13 is a pre-filing checklist.

Throughout, we speak in Judicial Council form numbers (e.g. FL-100, FL-150, FL-142). Where a document is named, its form number is given alongside.

3

Determine the Attorney's Role First

Whose information appears on every pleading depends entirely on David's role in the matter. Settle this before drafting anything.

Attorney of record

David is counsel of record for the client.

Use DAVID's info

Consulting attorney

David advises only; not named on the pleading.

Use CLIENT's info

Mediator

David mediates for both parties.

CLIENT's info + "Mediated by David Bransky, Esq."
Always

Disclosures are prepared as two full sets — one per party (Petitioner and Respondent, or two Petitioners under a Joint Petition).

Careful

No attorney means one signature. When David is consulting or mediating (not the attorney of record), the party signs only in the party signature block. If Form Builder also copies the client's signature into the attorney signature line, delete that duplicate — the respondent/client signs once, not twice.

4

Opening a Case (Initial Filing)

Every LA County case-opening filing requires three documents:

  1. Petition — FL-100 (or Joint Petition, FL-700)
  2. Summons — FL-110 (or Joint Summons, FL-710)
  3. Family Law Case Cover Sheet — FAM-020 (LA County form)
  • If there are minor children, also file FL-105 (UCCJEA declaration).
Rule

FL-105 defaults: items 4 and 6 are "No" 99% of the time — check No on both unless David instructs otherwise (item 5 stays blank absent DV orders). Each parent completes and signs their own copy. Completed mediation example: Sample FL-105 (mediation).

2026 change

Under the new Joint Petition (FL-700) and Joint Summons (FL-710), both parties are Petitioners — there is no Respondent. These replace FL-100 / FL-110. Either party may be Petitioner 1 or 2, but the same person must be Petitioner 1 (or 2) on every document in the case.

5

Filing a Response

  • Response only: FL-120.
  • Response with minor children: FL-120 + FL-105 (UCCJEA).
  • A respondent filing needs only the response (and UCCJEA if children) — no petition, summons, or cover sheet.
6

Preliminary Declaration of Disclosure (PDD)

Rule

Mandatory in California — no exceptions, regardless of asset level. Prepare one full set per party.

FormDocument
FL-142Schedule of Assets and Debts
FL-150Income and Expense Declaration
FL-140Declaration of Disclosure — 2-page form; the builder outputs 1 page, so always add page 2. Use real names.
FL-141Declaration Regarding Service of Declaration of Disclosure — the only PDD form filed with the court.

Workflow: draft in Form Builder → refine in Adobe → highlight everything for David's review. The paralegal is the first person to review the client's entries; never assume David already knows the context.

FL-142 — Schedule of Assets and Debts (4-page form)

  • The checking-accounts field is very small; if entries overflow, use an attachment form.
  • Common client errors to catch: IRAs entered in the 401(k)/pension section (that section is 401(k)s and pensions only); brokerage/stock accounts placed under "other assets."

FL-150 — Income and Expense Declaration

  • Page 1: statistical (age, tax-filing status). Page 2: income — W-2 wages, corporate reasonable compensation, investment income, or self-employment net of a P&L. Page 3: expenses. Page 4: only if minor children (parenting time).
  • Page 3 expenses — always check "estimated expenses." Clients routinely omit clothing, entertainment, gifts, and vacation; flag those.
  • Use asterisks "See Schedule of Assets and Debts" where applicable (added in Adobe).
Stop

Totals do not auto-calculate in Adobe. After any edit, manually re-add and verify every total before the form leaves your hands.

FL-141 — Declaration Regarding Service

  • Boxes (99% of matters): "other party or other party's attorney" + "via email." Mail is rare.
  • Signed by David (attorney of record) or by the client (consulting).

Statements labeling

  • Label each statement "As of [date]." Record each account by name and last 4 digits (Acct ####).
7

Corroborating Documents

  • Attach beneath FL-150 in Adobe: pay stubs (wage earners) or profit & loss (self-employed). Nothing attached if the party is not working (homemaker/retired).
  • Organize FL-142 support one of two ways (whichever David prefers):
    • Blue interstitial divider pages behind FL-142, labeled by category (e.g. Section 1 Real Estate), with deeds/statements filed behind each; or
    • A separate OneDrive subfolder "[Last] [First] PDD Corroborating Documents," with each file renamed to its FL-142 category number.
  • Reconcile every figure against the statements (e.g. form says $25,000, statement says $15,000 → highlight).
Rule

Detail, detail, detail. Clients make mistakes constantly — the highlight function is your best friend for surfacing them.

Judgment Package

The set of pleadings that settles the case — the judgment memorializes the full agreement (property division, support, custody, name change, etc.). The same form set is used for all three role types; only the name/info block changes.

Always

FormNotes
FL-180Judgment (sits on top of the stipulated judgment). If minor children, attach FL-192 behind it — nothing to complete on FL-192.
FL-190Notice of Entry of Judgment — no signatures; addresses on file with the court.
FL-130Appearance, Stipulation and Waivers.
FL-170Declaration for Uncontested Dissolution — complete sections 6 & 7 if there are children.

Usually

  • FL-144 — Stipulation and Waiver of Final Declaration of Disclosure (when disclosure is waived).

Sometimes — only on David's instruction

  • FL-195 · FL-435 · FL-455 · FL-191. Add only when David tells you to.
Rule

On FL-180, check the two service boxes — proof of service of summons or notice and acknowledgment of receipt. If a Response (FL-120) was filed (an appearance), check the appearance box. When more than one date applies, always use the earlier service date.

Per matter

A parentage case (unmarried parents with a child) uses a different box on FL-130. Confirm with David per case: name change, spousal support (reserve vs. terminate), attorney's fees, and who signs.

9

Spousal or Domestic Partner Support

When completing or reviewing the Spousal or Domestic Partner Support section, confirm the selected options are internally consistent for each party.

OptionSelect when…
Support PayableThe court is ordering spousal or domestic partner support payable to the identified party.
Terminate SupportThe court is terminating its jurisdiction to award support to the identified party. Once jurisdiction is terminated, that party generally cannot seek an award of support in the future.
Reserve SupportThe court is retaining jurisdiction over support for the identified party, so the issue may be determined at a later date.
  • "Support Payable" and "Reserve Support" generally should not both be selected for the same party — support is either being awarded now, or the issue is being reserved for future determination.
  • It is appropriate for "Terminate Support" to be selected for one party while "Support Payable" or "Reserve Support" is selected for the other (e.g. reserve for her, terminate for him).
Quality check

If both "Support Payable" and "Reserve Support" are selected for the same party, flag the document for review before proceeding. Do not assume both selections are intentional.

E-Filing (Green Filing)

  1. Confirm the role and select the correct existing case (it should populate).
  2. Upload the pleading from the client's OneDrive Pleadings subfolder.
  3. Set document security to "FL General Family Law" (LA requirement).
  4. Service contact: David (add the paralegal only if authorized).
  5. Pay with the business credit card; verify, then report the exact cost to David for client billing.
  6. Submit — David's email receives the filing confirmation.

Response/appearance fee ≈ $465–$467; verify the current amount before filing.

11

Client Communication & File Sharing

  • Email subject convention: "[Last Name]: [short description]" so everything is searchable.
  • Disclosure request email: send the blank FL-150 and blank FL-142 (with the firm's standard notes).
  • Create a disclosure subfolder "[Last].[First] Disclosure Docs" and share the OneDrive link in the email.
  • Using a Share link (no upload alerts): add "Please let us know when you upload documents." Using Request Files (auto-alerts; clients can't see each other's uploads): that sentence isn't needed.
Filing right

Pleadings vs. correspondence — file them in the right folder. Pleadings are court documents (e.g. the Joint Petition, Joint Summons, Family Law Case Cover Sheet, FL-105s) → Pleadings folder. Correspondence is letters and emails → Correspondence folder. Court forms are never correspondence.

12

Firm-Wide Standards

  • Highlight is your best friend — mark every fill-in, mistake, oddity, and reconciliation gap so David can review at a glance.
  • Never assume David has the context; the paralegal is the first reviewer of the client's work.
  • Manually verify all math after any Adobe edit.
  • Use clients' real full names — never placeholders.
  • Fonts and capitalization: on every form, keep fonts consistent and use proper capitalization — entries shouldn't drift between ALL-CAPS, lowercase, or mixed styles across fields or between documents in a packet.
  • Reference guides/templates: the Talerty file and prior mediation files in the drive.
